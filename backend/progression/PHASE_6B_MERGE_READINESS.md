## Verified implementation boundaries

- Isolated trusted progression guards, canonical event hashing, attribution, transactional reservation/recovery prototypes and regression suites exist. The pure account-owned single-choice reservation planner derives its projection and award delta from a strictly parsed choice and server-loaded ledger, and the successive-choice regression is in CI. The planner does **not** authenticate a session, reserve or persist an event, or make a player-ready endpoint. Review the Mongo integration tests' exact scope; do not infer live integration.
- An unregistered `/api/anonymous/credentialed-save` router creates a new server-generated anonymous identity and credential. An unregistered `/api/me/claim-story-with-credential` router verifies bearer proof within the MongoDB claim transaction and copies story-only state to an authenticated account. HTTPX/ASGI tests exercise issuance and claim against disposable MongoDB. A separate real-Chromium test uses browser `fetch` against localhost test-only FastAPI and a disposable MongoDB replica set, covering wrong proof, successful claim, same-account replay and second-account denial. **The browser test injects account identity; it does not exercise actual login, production UI or live routes.**
- Opt-in credentialed anonymous read/write helpers and frontend clients exist, but the browser's identity lifecycle and live `AuthContext` are not connected to them. A disposable-Mongo regression covers credentialed write followed by claim, stale revision, reward stripping and post-claim write denial. The write helper checks proof before revision and narrative validation, uses revision/credential/claim CAS and strips rewards; it **does not validate that a submitted narrative snapshot follows legitimate story choices**. The claim helper can preserve that unverified narrative. The trusted choice checkpoint guard is not connected to this write path and does not yet support all terminal and achievement-awarding transitions.
- Existing anonymous saves have no claim proof and cannot be silently backfilled based on `playerId` or revision. The existing `/api/me/claim` and `use_anonymous` strategy remain the live account-linking path. Their client-derived rewards and nontransactional transfer must never initialize a trusted ledger.
- The agreed policy is to preserve existing players' narrative progress while starting authenticated account holders with a fresh, server-authoritative balance of **50 BloodCoins** and fresh achievements. Neither existing client rewards nor unverified narrative flags may become authoritative reward evidence.

## Merge-blocking acceptance work

1. **Define release scope explicitly.** A review-only merge of inert infrastructure is separate from declaring Phase 6B feature-complete. Do not relabel this PR feature-complete while routes and UI are disconnected.
2. **Anonymous proof lifecycle:** connect server-generated issuance, secure delivery and client persistence, authenticated anonymous reads/writes, safe rotation/revocation and recovery. Establish a non-impersonating legacy-save transition; player ID and revision alone cannot bootstrap proof. Protect proof from URL/log exposure and address HTTPS, XSS, CSRF, rate limiting and device loss.
3. **Atomic ownership and choice writes:** bind verified-session account identity, a server-loaded clean ledger, pinned trusted content, strict event validation, canonical event identity, unique event/revision reservations and transactional attribution in one server-owned operation. Enforce a sole ledger writer and durable retry/recovery semantics. Test simultaneous choice writes, claim, rotation, stale revision, cross-account replay and ambiguous commits against disposable transaction-capable MongoDB. A pure planner or pre-transaction verifier alone is insufficient.
4. **Conflict and lifecycle UX:** implement explicit account-versus-anonymous comparison, keep both saves until resolution, provide cancellation, offline/retry and second-device restore. Prevent local auto-push and mode switches after failed or unresolved claims; test logout/account switching.
5. **Trusted narrative and progression cutover:** replace arbitrary client narrative snapshots with server-owned, content-version-pinned choice events and validated transitions. Define a separate legacy-story preservation policy without treating legacy flags as verified choices or reward eligibility. Implement authenticated event persistence, opening grant, achievement metadata, terminal/sequel/replay rules, and ensure browser reward fields never initialize or mutate the authoritative ledger.
6. **Release verification:** complete the full changed-file review (112 files at `c8b4c3e`) and real-login browser-to-API-to-disposable-Mongo flows. Review indexes, transaction support, secrets, observability, rollback and existing-player migration. Confirm all required CI checks on the final HEAD and compare with current `main`; arrange security and product review before activation.
