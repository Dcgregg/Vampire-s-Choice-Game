**Status: NOT ready to merge as a completed, playable Phase 6B.** This is a review record, not a production activation plan or permission to merge. The PR remains a draft of inert infrastructure. All five Phase 6B workflows passed on commit `3a076a75c2b95c92425831e7be5932f954accfd6` before this documentation update; CI for the final HEAD must be checked separately. Passing isolated tests does not establish end-to-end safety or production readiness.

## Verified implementation boundaries

- Isolated trusted progression guards, attribution, transactional prototypes and regression suites exist. Review their actual code and CI coverage; do not infer live integration.
- An unregistered `/api/anonymous/credentialed-save` router creates a new server-generated anonymous identity and credential. An unregistered `/api/me/claim-story-with-credential` router verifies the bearer proof within the MongoDB claim transaction and copies story-only state to an authenticated account. An HTTP test exercises issuance and claim against disposable MongoDB using an ASGI test client; this is **not** a real-browser end-to-end test.
- Opt-in credentialed anonymous read/write helpers and frontend clients exist, but the browser's identity lifecycle and live `AuthContext` are not connected to them. The write helper checks proof before revision and narrative validation, uses revision/credential/claim CAS and strips rewards; it **does not validate that a submitted narrative snapshot follows legitimate story choices**. The claim helper can preserve that unverified narrative. The trusted choice checkpoint guard is not connected to this write path and does not yet support all terminal and achievement-awarding transitions.
- Existing anonymous saves have no claim proof and cannot be silently backfilled based on `playerId` or revision. The existing `/api/me/claim` and `use_anonymous` strategy remain the live account-linking path. Their client-derived rewards and nontransactional transfer must never initialize a trusted ledger.
- The agreed policy is to preserve existing players' narrative progress while starting authenticated account holders with a fresh, server-authoritative balance of **50 BloodCoins** and fresh achievements. Neither existing client rewards nor unverified narrative flags may become authoritative reward evidence.

## Merge-blocking acceptance work

1. **Define the release scope explicitly.** A review-only merge of inert infrastructure is a separate decision from declaring Phase 6B feature-complete. Do not relabel this PR feature-complete while routes and UI are disconnected.
2. **Anonymous proof lifecycle:** connect server-generated issuance, secure delivery and client persistence, authenticated anonymous reads/writes, safe rotation/revocation and recovery. Establish a non-impersonating legacy-save transition; player ID and revision alone cannot bootstrap proof. Protect proof from URL/log exposure and address HTTPS, XSS, CSRF, rate limiting and device loss.
3. **Atomic ownership:** keep credential and revision verification inside the same MongoDB transaction as account insert and ownership stamp. Test simultaneous claim, rotation, stale revision, cross-account replay and ambiguous commit outcomes against a disposable transaction-capable deployment. A pre-transaction verifier is insufficient.
4. **Conflict and lifecycle UX:** implement explicit account-versus-anonymous comparison, keep both saves until resolution, provide cancellation, offline/retry and second-device restore. Prevent local auto-push and mode switches after failed or unresolved claims; test logout/account switching.
5. **Trusted narrative and progression cutover:** replace arbitrary client narrative snapshots with server-owned, content-version-pinned choice events and validated transitions. Define an explicit separate legacy-story preservation policy without treating legacy flags as verified choices or reward eligibility. Implement authenticated event persistence, opening grant, achievement metadata, terminal/sequel/replay rules, and ensure browser reward fields never initialize or mutate the authoritative ledger.
6. **Release verification:** complete the full changed-file review and real-browser-to-API-to-disposable-Mongo flows. Review indexes, transaction support, secrets, observability, rollback and existing-player migration. Confirm all required CI checks on the final HEAD and compare with current `main`; arrange security and product review before activation.

## Immediate safety rule

Do not register the older story-only route with a verifier that trusts only `playerId`, revision or a pre-transaction read. Do not connect the credentialed write path as a trusted narrative-event API until choice eligibility is enforced. Do not silently backfill credentials for legacy saves. Do not merge, deploy, migrate player data, initialize real ledgers or award real players on the strength of this document or green isolated tests.
