# Story-only account claim: cutover gates

Status: **not activated**. `story_claim_routes.make_story_claim_router` is not registered in `backend/server.py`; `linkAnonymousStoryOnly` is not called from `AuthContext`. The existing `/api/me/claim` route and its `use_anonymous` overwrite strategy remain in use. Do not interpret isolated green tests as a safe production cutover.

## Required before activation

1. **Anonymous ownership proof.** The new route accepts a `playerId` and expected revision, but possession of a `vc_...` identifier and knowledge of its revision are not strong proof of ownership. Design a server-issued, unguessable claim credential bound to the anonymous save, with safe issuance, persistence, rotation, and legacy-player migration. Verify it server-side without putting it in a public URL or logging it. Test stolen/guessed IDs, missing credentials, replay, and account-to-account claim attempts. Do not enable this route without that proof.
2. **Conflict decision and preservation.** A pre-existing account save must not be silently overwritten. Design the player-facing comparison using authenticated account and anonymous saves, with clear choices and a cancellation path. Preserve both saves until the chosen action succeeds. Review the current legacy `use_anonymous` route before retaining or replacing its overwrite behavior.
3. **Session and save lifecycle.** Use only verified server session identity. Account for a new device without a local anonymous save, an offline device with unsynced progress, and a stale anonymous revision. Do not enter account sync mode or auto-push local data after an unresolved claim conflict or transient failure. Confirm logout does not leak one account's story to the next user of the device.
4. **Trusted rewards isolation.** The account story transfer strips browser-derived `bloodCoins`, achievements, streaks, and entitlement-like fields. Never initialise the authoritative ledger from the transferred player state. Test nested story fields against the real content schema before deciding what is safe to preserve. The separate opening grant is not activated by a story claim.
5. **Database and deployment.** The claim service uses MongoDB transactions: require and verify transaction-capable replica-set/sharded deployment and unique account-save constraints. Run failure-injection and concurrent-claim integration tests against a disposable equivalent environment. Plan rollback, observability, and recovery for legacy saves before registering the route.
6. **End-to-end player verification.** Test anonymous play -> sign-in -> explicit conflict resolution -> second-device restore, including failed network calls and retry, without data loss or reward duplication. Verify frontend type checks, unit tests, backend integration tests, and deployment-specific smoke tests.

Only after these gates are met should a reviewed, explicit switch from the legacy claim path be proposed. Do not merge, deploy, migrate player data, or award real players as part of this document.
