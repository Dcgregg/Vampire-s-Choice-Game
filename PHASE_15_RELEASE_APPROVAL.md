# Phase 15 — Release-candidate approval

Phase 15 records an explicit human approval after a draft has passed its
editorial checks. It does not publish content.

1. Check a draft, then mark it **Ready for review**.
2. Select **Approve release candidate**.
3. The approval records the administrator, time and approved revision.
4. Any change to the draft details or scenes immediately clears approval and
   returns the draft to `draft` status.

Approved drafts can still be exported as review JSON. The approval route has no
access to the trusted player-story registry or player-save collections.
